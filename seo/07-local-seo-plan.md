## 1. Google Business Profile (do this first)

### 1.1 Set-up and verification

1. Go to business.google.com with the clinic's Google account (saishreepolyclinic@gmail.com).
2. Search for the business first — if a listing already exists (Google sometimes creates one), **claim it**
   rather than creating a duplicate.
3. **Business name:** exactly `Sai Shree Polyclinic`. Do not add keywords such as "Best Clinic Cuttack" —
   it breaks Google's rules and can get the listing suspended.
4. **Address:** Street `Kathagola Rd, near Shreema Hospital` · Area `Mangalabag` · City `Cuttack` ·
   PIN `753001` · State `Odisha`. Then **drag the map pin exactly onto the clinic entrance.**
5. Verification is usually by video: record continuously from the street, showing the signboard with the
   clinic name, the entrance, the reception, and proof of management (keys / staff-only area / registration
   certificate). The signboard name must match the GBP name.
6. Once verified, copy the pin's latitude/longitude and send them to the developer — they go into the
   website schema (`geo`, see seo/05-schema.md).

## 4. Five local link & PR ideas

1. **Free screening camp on World Diabetes Day (14 Nov)** — sugar and HbA1c screening with diet advice.
   Publish an event page on the website, post it on GBP, and send a short press note (with photos, no
   patient faces) to Odia dailies' Cuttack pages (e.g. Sambad, Dharitri, The Samaja, Prameya) and local news
   portals. Coverage usually links or names the clinic.
2. **Health talks at schools and colleges** — oral hygiene (Dental), asthma and smoke (Pulmonology),
   menstrual health and PCOS (Gynaecology). Institutions often post event reports on their websites and
   social pages, linking to the clinic.
3. **A doctor-written health column in Odia newspapers** — monthly 400-word pieces by your specialists on
   seasonal topics (Kartika fasting, summer kidney stones, winter COPD). The byline mentions the doctor, the
   clinic and the website.
4. **Industry and PSU empanelment** — apply to be empanelled for employee health with large employers whose
   staff live within reach (e.g. units around Paradip and the Angul–Talcher belt). Empanelled-facility lists
   are published on their websites — trusted local links plus a stream of patients. Check eligibility first.
5. **Insurance and scheme networks** — the clinic already offers insurance-linked billing for select
   providers. Make sure those insurers/TPAs list the clinic in their online network locators, and check
   eligibility for state schemes. These locator pages rank for "[insurer] network hospitals Cuttack".

Also worth doing: Rotary/Lions club health-camp partnerships, and Cuttack community pages and groups for
announcements (no spam — share genuinely useful information).
